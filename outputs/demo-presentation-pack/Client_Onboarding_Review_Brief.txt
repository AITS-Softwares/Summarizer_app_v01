NORTHSTAR FINANCE GROUP
Client onboarding review brief
Fictional Demonstration Data - prepared for product presentation only

Purpose
Northstar Finance Group is preparing a credit and operating-risk review for a proposed middle-market client, Meridian Components Ltd. The review team has collected onboarding notes, a preliminary portfolio extract, and a risk register. This brief gives the review questions that should be answered from the supporting material.

Requested outcome
Create a concise executive summary for the Credit Review Committee. The summary should describe the client profile, key financial and operating exposures, material open risks, and recommended actions before approval.

Client profile
Meridian Components Ltd. is a fictional manufacturer of precision assemblies serving transport, energy, and industrial customers. Revenue is concentrated in three customer groups and the business uses a revolving working-capital facility for inventory purchases and seasonal receivables.

Review themes
1. Revenue concentration: The three largest customer groups account for 58% of annual revenue. Assess whether concentration is acceptable and identify any monitoring actions.
2. Liquidity: A temporary inventory build is expected before the Q4 delivery cycle. Review the working-capital facility utilisation and any covenant sensitivity.
3. Financial controls: Monthly management accounts are available, but the August reporting pack was delivered nine business days late. Determine whether this affects reliance on current information.
4. Supply-chain resilience: Two critical component suppliers are located in the same geographic region. Identify the contingency evidence required before approval.
5. Data completeness: The current risk register includes items owned by Credit, Operations, Legal, and Relationship Management. Highlight any overdue or unassigned actions.

Decision criteria
- Escalate any open high-severity item with material financial exposure.
- Require a documented mitigation owner and target date for every material risk.
- Flag the application for committee review if projected covenant headroom falls below 15%.
- Recommend conditions precedent where information is incomplete, stale, or not independently verified.

Suggested prompts for the demo
- Summarize the attached materials for a credit committee in five bullets.
- Identify the three most important risks and their recommended mitigations.
- Compare the onboarding brief with the risk register and point out missing controls.
- Produce an action tracker with owner, due date, and escalation priority.

Confidentiality note
All names, figures, exposures, and operational details in this file are fictional. They are designed solely to demonstrate document ingestion, analysis, and reporting workflows.
